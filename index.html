Dr. Anusri Adusumilli, MBBS, MD

Consultant General Physician

Medical Registration No. APMC/FMR/94201 (Telangana State Medical Council)

Dr. Anusri Adusumilli is a Consultant General Physician at Edge Clinic, Hitech City, with 6.5 years of experience in general medicine. She manages infectious diseases, diabetes, hypertension and thyroid disorders, and cares for older adults and patients with more than one health condition. She also provides health screening and adult vaccinations.

Her consultations are built on careful listening, a thorough clinical examination, and a clear explanation of the diagnosis, the nature of the illness and its outlook, so that patients can make informed decisions about their treatment. She has neurology training from Apollo Hospital and JIPMER.

Languages: Telugu, English, Hindi and Kannada.
