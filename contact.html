Dr. Anusri Adusumilli is a Consultant General Physician at Edge Clinic, Hitech City. She sees adult patients with fever and infections, diabetes, blood pressure, thyroid problems, and with several health conditions at once. She also provides health check-ups and adult vaccinations.

You can walk in during clinic hours or consult online. Each consultation begins with listening to your concerns and a clinical examination, followed by a clear explanation of the diagnosis and the next steps. If you have previous reports or a list of your current medicines, please bring them with you. Consultations are for adults only. Paediatric care is not provided.
