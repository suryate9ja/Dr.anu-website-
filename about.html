Clinical Background

Dr. Anusri Adusumilli is a dedicated Consultant General Physician based in HITEC City, Hyderabad. With over 6.5 years of post-MD clinical experience, she specializes in managing acute infectious illnesses, chronic metabolic disorders (such as diabetes, hypertension, and thyroid imbalances), multimorbidity medication reviews, and geriatric care.

Her clinical training bridges internal medicine and neurological diagnostics. She completed senior clinical appointments including Senior Resident in the Department of General Medicine at Apollo Institute of Medical Sciences (Hyderabad), Senior Resident in Neurology at JIPMER (Puducherry), and Academic Registrar in Neurology at Apollo Hospitals (Jubilee Hills). In 2024, she pursued international post-doctoral training in Clinical Neurophysiology, EEG, and Epilepsy at the Cleveland Clinic (The Epilepsy Center), Ohio, USA.

Care Focus & Practice Scope

  • Acute Illnesses: Precise diagnosis for viral fevers, seasonal influenza, dengue/malaria workup, acute respiratory infections, and gastrointestinal illnesses.
  • Chronic Disease Management: Proactive long-term monitoring for Type-2 Diabetes, Hypertension, Dyslipidemia, and Thyroid hormone stabilization.
  • Multimorbidity & Polypharmacy: Reconciliation of medications for patients managing multiple simultaneous health conditions.
  • Preventive & Senior Health: Evidence-based adult vaccinations, annual screenings, and tailored geriatric health assessments.
Patient Scope Notice: Dr. Anusri specializes strictly in Adult and Geriatric Medicine (ages 18 and older). Paediatric consultations are not provided.
