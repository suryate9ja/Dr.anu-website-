Detailed Medical Scope

Conditions Evaluated & Treated

Organized clinical categories reflecting Dr. Anusri's scope of internal medicine practice.

Infectious & Acute Illnesses

Prompt investigation and evidence-based clinical management for acute bacterial, viral, and seasonal conditions.

  • Viral Fevers, Dengue, Malaria & Typhoid Workup
  • Cough, Seasonal Influenza, Bronchitis & Pharyngitis
  • Gastroenteritis, Food Poisoning & Acute Diarrhoea
  • Urinary Tract Infections (UTI) in Men & Women
  • Acute Sinusitis, Headache & Seasonal Allergies

Diabetes & Chronic Metabolic Care

Long-term metabolic regulation, insulin and oral hypoglycemic titration, and organ preservation.

  • Type-2 & Type-1 Diabetes Glycemic Management
  • Pre-diabetes & Metabolic Syndrome Reversal Guidance
  • HbA1c Target Titration & Glucose Monitoring
  • Diabetic Kidney & Neuropathy Protection Screenings
  • Lipid Imbalances, Hypercholesterolemia & Fatty Liver

Hypertension & Cardiovascular Health

Comprehensive blood pressure screening, vascular health preservation, and stroke risk mitigation.

  • Primary & Secondary Hypertension Evaluation
  • Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring Guidance
  • Cardiac Risk Stratification in Adults
  • Medication Adjustment for Controlled BP
  • Breathlessness & Palpitations Initial Workup

Thyroid & Endocrine Conditions

Detailed hormonal evaluation, thyroid hormone replacement, and chronic metabolic balancing.

  • Hypothyroidism (Underactive Thyroid) Dose Titration
  • Hyperthyroidism (Overactive Thyroid) Workup
  • Subclinical Thyroid Dysfunction & Antibody Tests
  • Unexplained Weight Fluctuations & Fatigue Workup
  • Calcium & Vitamin D / B12 Deficiency Corrections

Geriatric Care & Preventive Medicine

Dedicated senior citizen wellness, cognitive assessments, and recommended adult immunization.

  • Comprehensive Senior Citizen Routine Health Exams
  • Adult Immunizations (Influenza, Pneumococcal, Hepatitis, Tdap)
  • Cognitive Health, Forgetfulness & Memory Screenings
  • Frailty, Mobility & Fall Risk Evaluations
  • Periodic Annual Preventive Health Packages

Multimorbidity & Complex Diagnostics

Specialized investigative pathways for undiagnosed, complex, and unresolving health conditions in adults.

  • Multiple Co-existing Chronic Conditions Management
  • Polypharmacy & Medication Interaction Reconciliation
  • Persistent Unexplained Chronic Fatigue Investigations
  • Recurrent Fevers & Inflammatory Markers Workup
  • Specialist Referral Coordination & Unified Care Plans
